# Current status

The Rust/ash Vulkan prototype now has finite, destructible voxel soil. Automatic Grenades excavate real craters; Needle, Pulse and Satellite retain their roles. New runs start with Needle and Grenade, with three weapon slots total. Automatic combat, movement-dependent slow motion, eight waves, the final elite, shop, saved settings and shop checkpoints remain in place.

The arena is 64 by 64 metres, with 0.25 by 0.125 by 0.25 metre soil cells, 64 bit-packed eight-metre chunks, eight metres of initial soil beneath the clearing, protected bedrock and a stone perimeter. Existing terrain noise supplies initial elevations. Excavation removes solid cells and unsupported soil caps; ordinary crater rims are bevelled by removing additional soil. Exposed surfaces mesh synchronously from the collision state. Edited chunks and neighbors rebuild before presentation; no asynchronous queue can return stale results. Original schema-2 checkpoints are validated and upsampled on load, preserving their excavated surfaces.

Player and enemies share a swept capsule controller with rounded-bottom contact, physical stepping, slope speed correction, bounded snapping and world-time gravity. Small stairs are traversable without discrete full-voxel rises, substantial drops cause falls, and steep walls block movement. Visual correction is bounded to 0.1 metre. Elevation-aware flow fields connect to actual poses near rims, use locally invalidated height/clearance caches and propagate routes over the bounded arena. Blocked, unreachable and trapped-region probes are reported. There is no jumping, fall damage, teleport recovery or invisible ramp.

The 30-degree mouse-orbit camera follows elevation in real time. Five body probes drive a local dithered cutaway and cyan player outline with hysteresis and smooth transitions. Terrain and walls remain solid for movement and combat. Covered enemies do not gain the player's visibility treatment. Attempted player movement alone controls time scale; falling, camera motion and cosmetic debris do not accelerate it.

Schema-2 shop checkpoints persist voxel bits, column surfaces, physics parameters and body state. Loading validates solid/surface agreement and protected layers. Near-arena schema-1 checkpoints migrate to fresh voxel ground; historical distant saves outside the arena are rejected. Restarting restores initial terrain.

Actual verification is documented in [reports/VOXEL-TERRAIN.md](reports/VOXEL-TERRAIN.md). This remains a prototype: repeated excavation can genuinely trap characters and stall runs, and these cases are diagnosed rather than teleported away. Navigation is conservative around narrow exits and supports one walkable soil surface per column. Structures stay anchored at their authored elevations instead of settling. The renderer still uploads CPU-built triangles. Human movement feel, wider hardware testing and balance tuning remain necessary.
